About Me

I'm an Embedded Systems Engineer with a strong command of the full technology stack. While my foundation lies in designing and programming hardware systems, I've expanded my expertise across backend development, web engineering, data analytics, and graphic design — giving me the ability to build complete, end-to-end solutions from the hardware level all the way up to the user interface. I don't just write firmware; I architect the systems, analyse the data they generate, and design the platforms that bring them to life. My goal is to bridge the physical and digital worlds through technology that is efficient, scalable, and purposeful.
